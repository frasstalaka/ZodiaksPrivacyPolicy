## Summary

Zodiaks is an offline single-player game. It does **not** collect personal analytics, does **not** sell player data, and does **not** require an online account beyond Steam itself.

## Data stored on your device

The game may store the following locally on your computer (Godot `user://` path, typically under your OS application data folder for “Zodiaks”):

- Run save data (progress, equipped items, wave index)
- Meta progression unlocks
- Run history / career stats
- Settings (audio, graphics, language, input bindings)
- Optional player display name if you enter one
- Local error log files used only for debugging on your machine

Steam may also store purchase, playtime, and cloud-related metadata according to Valve’s own policies. That data is controlled by Steam, not by Zodiaks game code.

## Data we do not collect

As of this policy, the game does **not**:

- Send telemetry or analytics to our servers
- Upload crash reports automatically
- Use advertising trackers
- Access your contacts, camera, or microphone

## Children

Zodiaks is a general audience action game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Steam account requirements are governed by Valve.

## Third-party services

Distribution through Steam is subject to the [Steam Subscriber Agreement](https://store.steampowered.com/subscriber_agreement/) and Valve’s privacy policy.

## Your choices

You can delete local saves by removing the game’s user data folder on your device, or by using in-game delete actions where available. Uninstalling the game does not always delete `user://` save files.

## Changes

If we add online features (for example optional crash upload or Steam Cloud), this policy will be updated before those features ship.
